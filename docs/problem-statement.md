# Problem Statement

**Who:** A maintenance and operations planner at a mid-size manufacturer.

**Can't do what:** Find why machines go down, where spare parts should be stocked, which quality defects to catch early, or how to compare suppliers who send data in different formats.

**Because:** Operational data is scattered and slow to analyse.

**If this worked:** She would act before failures and shortages happen.

---

## The Four Problems

1. **Unplanned downtime** with no clear root cause
2. **Spare parts** held in the wrong location
3. **Quality issues** caught only at the end of the line
4. **Suppliers** reporting in inconsistent formats
